# Games

## [Caravan](/caravan.html)

Explore a (relatively) coherent LLM-generated world. Click around regions, subregions, and destinations to read their descriptions and see their pictures.

## [Astral Fugue](/llamaspace.html)

Travel the stars on a research mission and invent experiments for your crew, which an LLM plays out. You'll need an OpenRouter API key. The version here is an older demo; [the latest version is on itch.io](https://captdishwasher.itch.io/llamaspace).
